# Home Page Overrides

> **PROJECT:** My Shop Online
> **Page Type:** Storefront home
> Rules here **override** `MASTER.md`.

---

## Layout

- Full-bleed hero (edge-to-edge image). Not inset, not a card, not a side panel.
- First viewport only: **My Shop** (brand) + one headline + one supporting sentence + CTA group (`Mua ngay` + optional secondary) + product photo.
- No stats, promo chips, badges, or floating stickers on the hero image.
- Below the fold, one job per section: featured products → categories → latest posts. Each section: one headline + one short line.

## Color

- Page background: `#FFFFFF`.
- CTA: `#EA580C` / on-accent `#000000`.
- Do not paint the whole page mint.

## Motion

- Hero: one slow image ken-burns or fade (respect reduced motion).
- Product grid: stagger list from MASTER.
- No bento / feature-card showcase.

## Components

- `Header` + `Footer` live in storefront layout, not on this page.
- Product tiles are links with image + name + price — not dashboard cards.
